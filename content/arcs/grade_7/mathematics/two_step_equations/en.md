The math is: 200 + x + 100 = 250, where x is the cost of the upgrade.

## Stage 2: Concept
To solve a two-step equation, you must follow the Reverse Order of Operations (PEMDAS backwards). You isolate the variable by working backwards from the final number.

**Example:**  
3x + 7 = 16  
Step 1: Subtract 7 from both sides to undo the addition.  
Step 2: Divide by 3 to undo the multiplication.  

Inequalities use the same process but with inequality signs (like <, >, ≤, ≥). You solve them just like equations, except if you multiply or divide by a negative number, you must flip the inequality sign.

**Example:**  
2x + 5 < 15  
Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides  
Step 2: Divide by 2 (no flipped sign needed)  

To graph inequalities:  
- Use an open circle for > or <  
- Use a closed circle for ≤ or ≥  
- Shade the appropriate side of the number line

You also need to "check solutions" by substituting them back into the original equation or inequality to see if it is true.

## Stage 3: Guided
**Problem:**  
A car rental company charges a fixed fee of $10 plus $0.25 per kilometer driven. You paid $18.75 to rent the car. How many kilometers did you drive?  
Let x = number of kilometers driven  
Equation: 10 + 0.25x = 18.75  

**Checklist:**  
1. Subtract 10 from both sides  
2. Divide both sides by 0.25  

**Solution:**  
10 + 0.25x = 18.75  
0.25x = 8.75  
x = 35  

Check: 10 + 0.25(35) = 10 + 8.75 = 18.75 ✓

**MC Choices:**  
A) 25 km  
B) 35 km  
C) 45 km  
D) 55 km  

**Intervention Hints:**  
Level 1: What needs to be removed first?  
Level 2: What operation undoes addition?  
Level 3: When dividing, how can you avoid decimals?

## Stage 4: Practice

### Problem 1:
A class is ordering pizza. They’ve already spent $30 on sauce and toppings. Each pizza costs $12, and they need to buy 5 more pizzas to have enough. How much did the class spend in total?

Equation: 30 + 5 × 12 = x  
What is x?

A) 60  
B) 90  
C) 120  
D) 150  

**Intervention Hints:**  
Level 1: What is 5 × 12?  
Level 2: What do you add to 60 to get the total?  
Level 3: What's the total cost?

### Problem 2:
Sarah is saving money for a new bike. She already has $40 and saves $5 a week. She wants to save for 20 weeks. How much money will she have in total?

Equation: 40 + 5(20) = x  
What is x?

A) 90  
B) 100  
C) 140  
D) 200  

**Intervention Hints:**  
Level 1: What is 5 × 20?  
Level 2: What is 100 + 40?  
Level 3: What’s the total amount?

### Problem 3:
A movie theater has fixed seating charges of $8 and also charges $3 per ticket. If a group spends $29 for 5 tickets, how much did each ticket cost?

Equation: 8 + 3(5) = 29  
What would be the equation if each ticket had a $2 fee instead of $3?

A) 8 + 2(5) = 29  
B) 8 + 2 × 5 = 29  
C) 8 + 2 × 5 = x  
D) 8 × 2 + 5 = 29  

**Intervention Hints:**  
Level 1: What was the new ticket fee?  
Level 2: How much is 2 × 5?  
Level 3: What's the correct form of the new equation?

## Stage 5: Capstone

### Problem:
**Scenario:**  
Tania is planning a hiking trip to the Muskoka region. She needs to rent a tent and has a budget of $150. The tent costs $70 to rent, but she wants to leave room for snacks and water. She has a budget for an additional $30. How much can she spend on snacks, if she must subtract both the water and snacks from her total budget?

Let x = money spend on snacks  
Equation: 70 + x + 30 = 150  

She later realizes she can also rent a backpack for the trip, but it costs $35. If this brings her total spending to $185, how much is her backpack costing?

Let y = cost of backpack  
Equation: 70 + 30 + y = 185  

Answer:
1. Solve for x: 70 + x + 30 = 150  
   x = 50  

2. Solve for y: 70 + 30 + y = 185  
   y = 85  

Check your work using substitution!

**MC Choices for First Part:**

C) $60  
D) $70  

**MC Choices for Second Part:**

D) $90
