## Stage 2 – Concept

### Overview of Human Body Systems
Welcome to the world inside your body! Your body is made up of many parts called systems—just like a big machine that keeps you going every day. These systems work together to keep you healthy, strong, and alive.

#### Major Systems:
1. **Digestive System** – Breaks down food to get energy and nutrients.
2. **Circulatory System** – Carries blood, nutrients, and oxygen to all parts of the body.
3. **Respiratory System** – Helps you breathe in oxygen and get rid of carbon dioxide.
4. **Skeletal System** – Gives your body structure and support.
5. **Muscular System** – Lets your body move and help with everyday tasks.

#### How Systems Work Together:
They don’t work alone. For example, when you exercise:
- Your muscles work harder (muscular system),
- Your heart beats faster (circulatory system),
- You breathe more quickly (respiratory system),
- You need food and water for energy (digestive and circulatory system),
- And your bones support you (skeletal system).

#### Lifestyle Choices and Health:
What you eat, how much you sleep, what you do (exercise), and your stress levels all change how well your body systems work.

## Stage 3 – Guided Problem

### Guided Problem: When Exercise Makes Your Body Work Harder

**Problem:**  
When Sam plays hockey, his heart beats faster and he starts breathing quickly. Why does this happen?

A. His heart only needs to beat faster when his muscles work harder.  
B. His muscles need more oxygen when they work harder.  
C. His body doesn’t need energy when he exercises.  
D. His heart beats faster to help him think better.

**Correct Answer:** B. His muscles need more oxygen when they work harder.

**Explanation:**  
During exercise, your muscles need more energy to move. That energy comes from oxygen and nutrients that your blood carries. So, your heart beats faster to move more blood (and oxygen) to your muscles to keep them going.

**Intervention Hints for Guided Problem:**

- **Level 1:** Think of a time when your heart pounds fast, maybe after playing outside or climbing stairs.
- **Level 2:** Which system is responsible for carrying oxygen and nutrients to your muscles?
- **Level 3:** Try to imagine what happens if your muscles don’t get enough oxygen while working hard!

## Stage 4 – Practice Problems

### Practice Problem 1: What Does the Respiratory System Do?

**Problem:**  
Which of these best describes what the respiratory system does?

A. Gives shape and support to your body  
B. Carries blood and nutrients to your organs  
C. Helps you get oxygen and get rid of carbon dioxide  
D. Breaks down food to get energy  

**Correct Answer:** C. Helps you get oxygen and get rid of carbon dioxide  

**Intervention Hints:**

- **Level 1:** How do you breathe? Is it to take something in or to get rid of something?
- **Level 2:** Which system is your lungs part of?
- **Level 3:** When your lungs work harder (when running, for example), what do they help you do?

### Practice Problem 2: Which Systems Are Involved When You Eat?

**Problem:**  
When you eat lunch, which body systems help get the nutrients into your muscles?

A. Digestive and circulatory  
B. Skeletal and muscular  
C. Respiratory and nervous  
D. Endocrine and excretory  

**Correct Answer:** A. Digestive and circulatory  

**Intervention Hints:**

- **Level 1:** What part of your body helps you digest your sandwich?
- **Level 2:** What carries nutrients from the food to the rest of your body?
- **Level 3:** Try to think of how food moves through your body: first broken down (digestive), then carried (circulatory).

### Practice Problem 3: How Does Your Lifestyle Affect Your Body?

**Problem:**  
Which lifestyle choice would make the circulatory system work harder?

A. Sleep well every night  
B. Eat lots of fast food  
C. Exercise regularly  
D. Drink little water  

**Correct Answer:** B. Eat lots of fast food  

**Intervention Hints:**

- **Level 1:** What happens to your body when you eat unhealthy food?
- **Level 2:** What does fast food have in large amounts that could make your heart work harder?
- **Level 3:** Fast food often has salt and fat. Do these things require more work from your circulatory system?

## Stage 5 – Capstone

### Capstone Problem: Understanding How the Body Systems Work Together

**Problem:**  
Layla is training for a swimming competition. She feels tired and needs more energy. Her coach tells her she should eat a healthy breakfast and stay hydrated.  
Which body systems are involved in Layla getting energy and staying strong for training and why?

A. Digestive, Muscular, and Circulatory Systems  
B. Skeletal, Respiratory, and Nervous Systems  
C. Digestive, Respiratory, and Muscular Systems  
D. Muscular, Circulatory, and Excretory Systems  

**Correct Answers:**
1. Digestive — breaks down her food to provide energy.  
2. Circulatory — delivers that energy (as nutrients and oxygen) to her muscles.  
3. Muscular — uses the energy to move and keep her swimming strong.

**Correct Answer:** A. Digestive and Muscular Systems  

Wait, that’s not right. The real answer is:
**Correct Answer:** A. Digestive, Muscular, and Circulatory Systems  

But to make the capstone more challenging and accurate to the real systems involved:

Let’s correct and simplify:

**Correct Answers:**
1. **Digestive System** – Breaks down the food to get nutrients and energy.
2. **Circulatory System** – Carries these nutrients and oxygen to the muscles.
3. **Muscular System** – Uses the energy to move and keep the body working.

So, **A. Digestive, Muscular, and Circulatory Systems** is the best answer.

### Intervention Hints for Capstone:

1. **Level 1:** Layla is swimming, using her muscles. To make muscles work, they need energy. What do you need to get that energy?
2. **Level 2:** She must eat food and then her body gets nutrients. Who helps carry those nutrients through the body?
3. **Level 3:** What part makes her muscles move? Can you name all 3 systems that help her body prepare for exercise?

### Optional Alternate Capstone (If child struggles with Capstone):

**Alternate Problem:**  
After running, your body wants to cool down. Which body systems are involved in this cooling process?

A. Circulatory and Respiratory Systems  
B. Skeletal and Muscular Systems  
C. Digestive and Excretory Systems  
D. Nervous and Endocrine Systems  

**Correct Answer:** A. Circulatory and Respiratory Systems  

**Explanation:**  
When the body cools down:
- Your circulatory system sends blood near your skin to lose heat.
- Your respiratory system breathes faster to release heat.
